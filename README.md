## ✨ Key Features

1. **🛰️ Full-Screen 3D Exploded Vehicle Showcase**
   * High-contrast GPU-accelerated 10-second animation cycle with multi-layer depth extrusion.
   * Interactive 3D component hotspot targeting and camera preset perspective switches (*Drive Core, Battery Matrix, Exploded Blueprint*).

2. **🔍 "Will It Fit My EV?" Vehicle Compatibility Matcher**
   * Filter and verify compatible parts for *Ola S1/Air, Ather 450X, Hero Electric, Okinawa, Ampere, Pure EV, Bajaj Chetak, TVS iQube, and Custom 2W/3W* scooters.
   * Real-time `✓ Guaranteed Fit` badge.

3. **🔋 Interactive EV Battery & Range Simulator**
   * Interactive tuning for Voltage (48V / 60V / 72V), Capacity (20Ah – 50Ah), Motor Rating (800W – 2000W), and Riding Modes.
   * Real-time dynamic estimation of range (km), top speed (km/h), charging duration, and pack pricing in ₹.

4. **⚡ 1-Click WhatsApp Quick Order**
   * Pre-formatted WhatsApp quotation messages on every component card with instant vehicle fit question and starting price.

5. **🛒 Multi-Item Workshop Quotation Cart Drawer**
   * Mechanics and workshops can assemble multi-part orders with automatic bulk volume discounts (5% / 10%) and 1-click WhatsApp export.

6. **🗺️ Franchise ROI & Territory Calculator**
   * Investment tiers (*Starter Workshop, Authorized Dealer, Super Stockist*) with projected margins and district exclusivity status.

7. **⭐ Verified Workshop Reviews & Mechanic Testimonials**
   * Social proof and ratings from verified EV service centers across South India.

8. **⚙️ Web Owner WhatsApp & Store Customizer**
   * Change your official WhatsApp support number, Instagram handle, and store location anytime directly via `/admin`.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Wouter Router, TanStack Query
* **Backend:** Node.js, Express, Drizzle ORM, Zod Schema Validation, PostgreSQL
* **Deployment:** Vercel Production Infrastructure
